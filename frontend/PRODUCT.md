# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Hackathon judges (NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026) and curious non-experts who want to understand what happens to a forest after a wildfire. They arrive without remote-sensing knowledge, often during a live demo or a short self-guided visit, and should grasp the result visually before reading any explanation. A secondary audience is the team itself and fellow researchers checking how the evidence was built.

## Product Purpose

An interactive web application that shows how forests recover after wildfires, centred on NASA–ISRO's NISAR L-band radar. Its core message: a forest can look green again (optical greenness, NDVI) long before its structure has recovered. The user explores a fire, sees its current condition, compares satellite perspectives, follows a recovery timeline and sees what the future may hold.

Success: a viewer leaves understanding the difference between a green landscape and a structurally recovered forest, and trusts that every number shown is real, sourced and honestly labelled.

## Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, honest.

A mature scientific visualization platform, not a hackathon dashboard. Calm confidence: the data and the map carry the story; text is short and exact. Emotional goal is quiet wonder at the planet, followed by clarity about the evidence.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboard styling: boxed cards everywhere, rounded containers, KPI tiles.
- Excessive gradients, glows and glassmorphism.
- Random decorative circles, dots and particles that do not encode data.
- Large blocks of explanatory text.
- The conventional green "eco" website aesthetic.
- Visualizations that look impressive but do not communicate real data.
- Red versus green as the only way to tell recovery stages apart.

## Design Principles

1. **Accuracy before effect.** No visual choice may overstate what the data supports.
2. **Keep kinds of knowledge apart.** Observations, model estimates, forecasts and illustrations must always be visibly distinguishable.
3. **Missing stays missing.** Empty, unfetched or uncertain data is shown as such, never filled in or smoothed over.
4. **The map tells the story.** Spatial visualization and imagery carry more meaning than prose; text supports, it does not lead.
5. **One coherent journey.** Explore a fire, understand its condition, compare perspectives, follow the timeline, look ahead: every page should feel like part of that sequence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for text and essential graphics.
- Every animation respects `prefers-reduced-motion`; nothing is understood only through motion.
- Colour is never the sole carrier of meaning; no red/green encoding (colour-blind safe for deuteranopia and protanopia).
- Keyboard access and visible focus on every control; touch targets at least 44 px on coarse pointers.
