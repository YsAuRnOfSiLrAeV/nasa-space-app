# Product

## Register

product

## Users

NASA Space Apps visitors exploring forest recovery after historical fires.

## Product Purpose

Help visitors select a fire and understand its area and recovery using supplied geospatial data. Make modeled estimates distinct from measured observations.

## Brand Personality

Scientific, plain-spoken, exploratory.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense dashboard-style stat grids, repeated explanatory copy, promotional language, and claims that overstate the supplied data.

## Design Principles

- Foreground the selected fire and its modeled recovery.
- Keep primary controls visible and concise.
- Distinguish model estimates from measured observations.
- Preserve access to scientific details without crowding the main view.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard access and clear contrast.
